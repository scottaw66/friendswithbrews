Scott drinks Yamamotoyama's organic sencha green tea, and Peter cracks open a Harpoon Seaport After Midnight Stout, the bourbon-barrel-aged dessert stout Scott already liked on his Vermont visit.

Peter just got back from running the Athens Authentic Marathon. He walks through race morning, the buses out to Marathon, the piles of discarded ponchos and clothes, the long gradual climb, and a promised downhill that he swears he never felt. He finished in his second-best marathon time by about thirty seconds, passed a 75-plus runner doing the course barefoot in Spartan armor, and learned from the runner seated next to him on the flight home that the man is famous.

They also talk about Greek food, spanakopita versus Peter's dad's version, travel advice from YouTube that turned out to be wrong, the Acropolis Museum, the cats of Athens, Altra running shoes, race-photo facial recognition, and the girls on roller skates.
