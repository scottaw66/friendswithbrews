Scott's brother Keith joins the show. Scott has Laurelwood's Pumpkin Ale, brewed in Astoria, which tastes spicy to him. Keith has Talking Cedar's MMIW Tribal Pale Ale from the Chehalis Tribe in Washington, with a portion of the proceeds supporting Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women. Chloe the cat gets Tacoma tap water.

They talk about what they're each building with Claude, American short-term thinking, China and Europe in AI, how narrow education has become, why people are so sure everyone else is wrong, biology that looks designed, and whether a business has to be online at all.
